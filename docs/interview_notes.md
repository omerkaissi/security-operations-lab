**Why change the rule?** A five-failure threshold misses some four-failure sequences. The experimental 1.1 policy lowers that threshold while preserving windows and reset. On 12 self-authored holdout scenarios, baseline TP/FP/TN/FN was 2/2/5/3; revised was 4/3/4/1. It recovered H01 and H08 and introduced a benign H02 alert. This is a tradeoff on an authored set, not production performance. [Summary](../reports/strengthening/summary.json), [plan](strengthening_experiment.md).

**What does exact conformance mean?** Candidate rule/version, anchor and ordered evidence IDs match manually authored expectations. This checks specification behavior on the fixture; it does not establish security truth. `--check` returns nonzero for a mismatch, while a normal evaluation may complete despite mismatches. **What are the limits?** The holdout was self-authored by the experiment author, not independent or representative. Actor grouping does not prove causation; missing earlier events affect export-cap usage. No real incident, user contact, production deployment or measured business impact occurred. A clean committed checkout, publication privacy review and independent technical review remain outstanding. A profile with more alerts may create more false positives, even when conformance is perfect.
